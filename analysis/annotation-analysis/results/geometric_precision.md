## Interpretation
- **Area**: GSAM2 +3.8% / U-Net -1.5% (both small; consistent with radius_ratio 1.016 / 0.993).
- **Axes**: GSAM2 Dr ~-0.16/-0.22px (sub-pixel) vs U-Net -0.57/-0.55px (systematic under-sizing) -> U-Net's mask-shrink bias is the quantitative cause of its lower IoU/boundary.
- **Orientation**: both ~2.2-2.5deg median (elongated) -> comparable.
- **Boundary**: GSAM2 ASSD 0.53 vs U-Net 0.99px (~2x), d_H^95 1.37 vs 2.32 -> GSAM2 boundary clearly closer to GT.
- Metric (center) and Geometric (shape) independently pick GSAM2 as the most-precise independent estimator -> supports adopting GSAM2 as the auditor for label-uncertainty (sigma_label) at the shape level too.
